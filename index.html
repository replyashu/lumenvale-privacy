# Lumenvale Privacy Policy Last updated: 4 October 2026 This policy explains what information the Lumenvale app (package name com.replyashu.lumenvale) handles. The app is made by Ashutosh. ## What we collect We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no account sign-up, no advertising and no analytics. ## What stays on your device Your character name, appearance and game progress (coins, gems, homes, vehicles and items) are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. We cannot see or recover this data. ## In-app purchases Gem packs are sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and we never receive your card or payment details. The app receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play on your device, which it uses only to add the gems you bought. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment: https://policies.google.com/privacy ## Children The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. ## Changes If a future version adds online play or collects any information, we will update this policy and the date above before that version is released. ## Contact Questions about this policy: ashutoshsmartcool@gmail.com
